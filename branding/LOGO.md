# Bozkurtlar logosu

Araç: yerleşik image_gen, yeni üretim. Ana dosya: bozkurtlar-logo.png. Şeffaf arka planlı raster PNG; SVG değildir. Mobil uygulamaya ve platform uygulama simgelerine eklendi.

Kullanılan üretim istemi:

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: standalone app emblem for the Turkish student technology team Bozkurtlar, building a water-reuse and green-transformation monitoring project. Primary request: create one refined, original, compact wolf-head emblem integrating a water droplet and a subtle leaf through elegant negative space. A calm geometric wolf, clever balanced silhouette, contemporary technology and ecological identity, memorable and readable as a small mobile-app icon. Flat vector-like raster artwork, forest-green and turquoise ecological colors with a deep ink outline where needed, crisp solid shapes. Single centered emblem, generous transparent margin, no text, no letters, no mockup, no gradients, no watermark, no flags or political symbols. True transparent background.
